OpenAI-Backed Thrive Holdings Buys ADT in a $15.3 Billion Deal to Build an AI Smart-Home Security Giant

October 30, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 4 min read

The AI takeover of your front door is real: Thrive Holdings, the investment firm backed by OpenAI, has acquired ADT — one of America’s biggest home-security companies — for $10 per share, or about $7.6 billion in equity, in a deal with a total enterprise value of $15.3 billion. ADT will become the foundation of Thrive Holdings’ smart-home security business, which the firm plans to rebuild around AI.

What happened

Thrive Holdings and ADT announced the deal on Thursday, October 29, 2026. Thrive Holdings is the private-equity spin-out of Josh Kushner’s venture firm Thrive Capital, and it counts OpenAI as both a partner and an investor. The firm already holds more than 75 companies, and its model is to hold businesses long-term and revitalize them with AI. As part of the partnership, OpenAI supplies AI models and technology — plus people, in the form of forward-deployed engineers — to Thrive’s companies. Neither company has announced specific new AI-powered security products yet, but both teased that the plan is to invest in ADT and release new products.

Why it matters

The deal lands as AI-powered physical security heats up. Apple has just entered the field with a new line of smart-home and home-security products built with LG and Schneider Electric, and Amazon has updated its smart-home line, including new AI-powered home cameras. Startups are even pitching AI dog robots as roving security guards.

“ADT will form the foundation of Thrive Holdings’ smart home security business.” — Thrive Holdings / ADT announcement, via TechCrunch
In other words, the company that has guarded American homes for generations is about to get an AI brain — and the race to own the intelligent home is now a multi-billion-dollar land grab.

What it means for you

If you run a business that touches homes, property or security — installers, integrators, property managers, smart-home retailers — expect the baseline to move fast: AI cameras that understand what they see, fewer false alarms, and security that acts instead of just recording. For homeowners, the practical question is what happens to existing ADT systems and subscriptions as new AI products roll out; watch for announcements on upgrades rather than replacements. And for anyone building AI products, this is a reminder that the biggest AI deals of 2026 are not only about chatbots — they are about putting AI into the physical world.
