AI's Missing Ingredient Is Trustworthy Data: Context Labs Launches AI Distillery

November 2, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 3 min read

AI's missing ingredient is trustworthy data. Context Labs B.V. launched its AI Distillery platform on November 1, 2026, through the Microsoft Marketplace — a product that turns an enterprise's operational and business data into a governed, traceable Context Package that any AI model the customer selects can then use.

What happened

The AI Distillery maps structured and unstructured enterprise data to industry-specific ontologies and stores the result in a Context Package with cryptographic provenance, so every answer a model draws from it can be traced back to its source records. The platform checks answers against the company's own records before any model enters production, works with the frontier or open-weight model the customer chooses, and runs inside the customer's own data centre or chosen cloud. Each enterprise licence includes an industry starter kit, and Context Labs is demonstrating the platform on energy-sector use cases at ADIPEC 2026. The company has not published benchmark figures for accuracy or processing throughput in the available material — a gap worth noting for a product whose pitch is verifiability.

Why it matters

The framing Context Labs is betting on comes from Gartner: the research firm projects that a large share of agentic AI projects will be cancelled, and that AI projects lacking AI-ready data risk abandonment — figures the company cites to argue that data readiness, not model capability, is now the bottleneck. Whether or not those forecasts land exactly, the direction is hard to dispute: models keep getting stronger and more alike, while the data they are allowed to use — governed, traced, checked — is what differs between a demo and a deployment. Traceability turns an answer into evidence, and evidence is what regulated industries, auditors and procurement teams have been waiting for before they let AI near consequential decisions.

What it means for you

Before you buy a bigger model, audit your data: map where operational records actually live, how current they are, and who can vouch for them. When evaluating any AI vendor, demand source tracing for high-stakes answers and a pre-production check against your own records — the two features at the heart of this launch. A model swap is easy; untangling a data mess under production pressure is not. Governance, treated as a feature rather than paperwork, is becoming a buying criterion.
