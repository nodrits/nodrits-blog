OpenAI Eyes $30B Raise at $1.25T Valuation — The Most Valuable Private Company Ever

October 23, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 3 min read

A company worth more than a trillion dollars that has never gone public: OpenAI is reportedly working on raising up to $30 billion at a $1.25 trillion pre-money valuation in a round led by Coatue and investors in the UAE, according to sources cited by CNBC and reported by TechCrunch. If it closes at that price, the ChatGPT maker would break records as the most valuable private company in history.

What happened

TechCrunch reported on October 22 that OpenAI is close to securing another mega round of private funding instead of plowing ahead directly with an IPO. Sources told CNBC the round is being led by Coatue alongside UAE-based investors, with terms still being negotiated — so the final numbers could still change. CNBC also reported the UAE funding could be paired with a deal to secure additional computing capacity for OpenAI from the UAE. OpenAI declined to comment.

This follows a similar report by Bloomberg a month earlier that the lab was working on a $30 billion raise at a $1.4 trillion valuation. The shifting figures are normal at this stage of negotiation, but the direction is unmistakable: each new reported term sheet pushes the ceiling higher.

Why it matters

A valuation above $1 trillion would make OpenAI the most valued private company ever — and it would reset the bar for its forthcoming IPO. OpenAI filed confidential paperwork for an IPO in June, and its last raise in March valued the company at $852 billion post-money. Jumping from $852 billion to $1.25 trillion in months would be one of the steepest private-market climbs ever recorded.

The new price tag would also raise the bar for Anthropic's IPO, which many believe is closer to materializing — rumors suggest Anthropic's public S-1 filing could appear any day now. CNBC reports Anthropic is seeking a $1.5 to $2 trillion valuation for its public debut, after raising $65 billion at a $965 billion valuation in May and filing confidential IPO paperwork in June.

In short: the two AI labs are now racing each other toward public markets at valuations that would each rank among the largest companies on Earth.

What it means for you

If you run a business that depends on AI tooling — websites with AI chat, automation workflows, AI-assisted design — the message behind these numbers is simple: the infrastructure build-out is not slowing down. Trillion-dollar private valuations mean the labs can keep training bigger models and the compute race keeps accelerating. For buyers of AI services, that means more capable models, deeper integrations, and pricing pressure on everyone else trying to compete.

The Nodrits take

Record valuations don't arrive because a company is finished — they arrive because the market believes the biggest chapters are still ahead. OpenAI choosing one more mega private round over rushing its IPO suggests it wants maximum firepower before going public. The question for the rest of us: when the dust settles on this funding cycle, who actually owns the AI stack your business runs on?
