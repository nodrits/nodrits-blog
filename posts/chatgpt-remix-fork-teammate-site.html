ChatGPT Remix Lets Teams Fork a Teammate's Website in Chat

November 8, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 3 min read

Steal your teammate's website — in one chat. OpenAI began rolling out ChatGPT Remix on November 6, 2026: a feature that copies a teammate's ChatGPT Site so you can change your own version just by describing the edits in chat.

What happened

OpenAI announced Remix in a post on X from its ChatGPT account on November 6, 2026, with a short demo video. Remix is a ChatGPT Sites feature: spot a teammate's site, make your own copy of it, tell ChatGPT what you would change, and keep editing your own version in the same chat — the original site is left untouched. It is rolling out to ChatGPT Business and Enterprise workspaces only. Personal plans — Free, Go, Plus and Pro — are not included in this rollout, and OpenAI has not said when, or whether, it will reach them. There is no separate price for Remix: it arrives as part of the workspace plan, with Business Standard seats at $20 per user per month billed annually or $25 monthly. OpenAI has not published a Help Center page for Remix, or said how remixed sites handle the original's database, and has not given a date by which every Business and Enterprise workspace will see it.

Why it matters

Websites are becoming remixable team assets. Start from a colleague's page instead of a blank prompt, and the AI website race moves from generation to collaboration — one person's site becomes the whole team's starting point. The pattern is familiar: Anthropic merged Claude chat and Cowork into one screen in September, and OpenAI is now merging its own Chat and Work modes. The assistant is becoming the place where work lives, not just where questions get answered. Remix is the website-shaped version of that shift: a site is no longer a finished artifact, it is a living document your team can fork, edit and ship.

What it means for you

If you run a Business or Enterprise workspace, try Remix on a low-stakes page first — a landing page or an internal tool — and check what happens to the original's database before you fork anything that matters. For everyone else, this is a wait-and-watch: no date for personal plans, no published feature list, and no clarity on database handling yet. If you are choosing a workspace plan, Remix is now one more reason the Business tier earns its seat price.
