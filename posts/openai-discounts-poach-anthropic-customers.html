OpenAI Is Buying Anthropic's Customers With Discounts Up to 90 Percent

October 26, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 4 min read

OpenAI is reportedly pulling Anthropic’s biggest customers across with discounts of up to 90 percent. The Information reports that Replit shifted a contract worth more than $100 million a year after OpenAI offered over 30 percent off — and Rippling’s coding workload flipped from 57 percent Anthropic to 52 percent OpenAI in five months.

What happened

According to The Information’s reporting, relayed by The Decoder, OpenAI offered Replit more than 30 percent off a contract worth over $100 million a year after Anthropic declined to extend Replit’s earlier discount without a higher spending commitment. Replit CEO Amjad Masad said on stage at TechCrunch Disrupt that the company was no longer working with Anthropic, later softening that to OpenAI being "our preferred partner for powering Replit Agent." Replit’s documentation still lists seven Claude models, and OpenAI is now the default for Replit Agent.

Rippling CEO Parker Conrad said OpenAI’s share of his company’s coding work rose from 16 percent in May to 52 percent, while Anthropic’s fell from 57 to 18 percent, because OpenAI turned out cheaper. DataCamp’s CEO said OpenAI pricing cut his costs by 80 to 90 percent, and a US bank with over $7 billion in assets reportedly doubled its ChatGPT seats to 500 at no extra charge, paying under $7 per user against a usual $20 or more. Neither OpenAI nor Anthropic has confirmed the figures.

Why it matters

This is the moment AI models became swappable commodities at the application layer. When a coding product can switch providers and keep working, a 30 percent price gap outweighs a small capability gap — and the moat moves from the model to the contract. The timing is brutal for Anthropic, which is reportedly preparing a large IPO as soon as next month while an investor memo says its growth slowed from Q2 to Q3. Cheaper models from DeepSeek and Kimi add pressure from below.

What it means for you

Treat model providers as negotiable, not permanent. Benchmark your own workload instead of trusting marketing charts, lock pricing terms rather than model names, and build a switch path before you need one. The discount you negotiate today is worth more than the benchmark lead you rent.
