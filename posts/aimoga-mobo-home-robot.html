A Car Company Just Opened Global Pre-Orders for a Home Robot

October 23, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 3 min read

A car company just started selling a robot for your living room. AiMOGA Robotics — the embodied-AI brand of Chinese automaker Chery — opened global pre-orders for Mobo, its first home companion humanoid, at a launch event in Wuhu, China on Oct 21, 2026.

What happened

Mobo is a 0.9-meter-tall robot with 22 degrees of freedom, running AiMOGA's "MoAgent" agent system, which breaks long-horizon tasks into steps. It has home spatial awareness, long-term memory, FaceID-style family-member recognition, 20+ language support, and can handle 16 tasks in parallel. It's aimed at elderly companionship, kids' companionship (reminders, stories, English practice) and whole-family use. Chery says Mobo drew interest from more than 10,000 prospective users since its August debut, cumulative commercial robot deliveries have passed 3,000 units across 60+ countries, and it plans 300 experience centers worldwide. Price and delivery dates were not disclosed.

Why it matters

Automakers are racing into home robotics on the back of their supply chains — home robots are shifting from factory technology to consumer products, and the category just went mainstream.

What it means for you

A new platform wave means a new client market. Companion robots will need apps, integrations, content and services — watch for the first "robot app store" moment. Early movers win the service layer.

The Nodrits take

Tesla has Optimus, Figure has its bots, and now a carmaker is taking pre-orders for your living room. The hardware race is on — but the software layer (apps, integrations, services) is still wide open. That's where the opportunity is.
