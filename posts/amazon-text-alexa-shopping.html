Amazon Lets You Text Alexa to Shop — After Blocking Meta's Muse

November 9, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 3 min read

Amazon blocked Meta's shopping agent from its store in September. Now it is letting you text its own agent to shop. On November 6, 2026, Amazon announced that select US customers can text Alexa for Shopping to search for products, compare prices, add items to a cart and complete purchases — in plain natural language.

What happened

Amazon announced the feature on November 6, 2026, just before the holiday shopping season. It starts with a small group of invited US customers: they receive a text asking them to enable the feature, accept the terms, link their phone number, and start shopping. The contact appears in the phone as Alexa Shopping. Everyone else can join a waitlist, and Amazon says the feature will roll out to millions more customers in the coming weeks. The text agent builds on Alexa's recent shopping upgrades: it can manage lists, keep tabs on prices, search for items across the web, compare products, check order status, and search for similar listings from a photo of an item or an outfit. A request started over text carries over to the Amazon Shopping app, amazon.com and Alexa-enabled devices. Amazon says Alexa for Shopping was used by more than 400 million customers in the last 12 months, according to its third-quarter release. TechCrunch, which first reported the launch, notes the context Amazon's own announcement does not mention: Amazon blocked Meta's Muse agent from querying its site in September, and the new feature works much like you can with AI agents like Muse or Instinct.

Why it matters

The largest storefront in US e-commerce has now stated its position by action: outside agents do not get to shop here, but Amazon's own agent gets the most intimate channel there is — your text thread. DoorDash reached a similar conclusion last month, per TechCrunch's reporting: third-party agents had placed only hundreds of orders on its platform, so it is concentrating on its own text-based agent instead. The pattern for agent builders is blunt: distribution through someone else's storefront is a privilege that can be revoked, not a right. The agent wars have turned territorial — the fight is no longer about who has the smartest assistant, but about whose storefront an assistant is allowed to enter.

What it means for you

If you are a US Amazon customer, check whether you have been invited, or join the waitlist, and test the agent the way you would test any shopping agent: start with price tracking and list management, and review the cart yourself before any purchase. If you sell on Amazon, note that product discovery is moving into a text thread you do not control — your listings' titles, images and price accuracy are now what an agent reads on a customer's behalf. Get those right before the holiday rush, because the agent will not ask you twice.

The Nodrits take

Texting is the most intimate channel there is, and Amazon just claimed it for its own agent. The storefront decides which agents may shop there — that is the new rule of the agent economy.
