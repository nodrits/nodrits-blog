Apple Unveils M6 MacBook Pro and iMac: Faster On-Device AI, 24-Hour Battery, November 6 Launch

October 27, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 3 min read

Apple has unveiled a new 14-inch MacBook Pro and a new iMac, both powered by its latest M6 chip, pitching much faster AI performance run directly on the device. The MacBook Pro starts at $1,999 and the iMac at $1,499, with pre-orders opening on October 28 and availability from November 6.

What happened

Apple unveiled the two machines on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, a day before its “Pro and tell” event in Los Angeles on October 28. The 14-inch MacBook Pro runs the M6 chip, which Apple says delivers up to 30% faster graphics and up to 25% faster AI performance than its M5 predecessor, along with up to 24 hours of battery life — the longest ever in a Mac. The new iMac jumps from the M4 chip to M6, and Apple says it delivers up to 5x faster on-device AI performance than the previous M4 model. Pre-orders open on October 28 in 35 countries and regions, including the US, with availability starting November 6.

Why it matters

The launch keeps Apple’s pitch squarely on on-device AI: faster AI tasks without waiting on the cloud, in machines buyers already know. It also lands just ahead of the October 28 event, where Bloomberg News has reported Apple is expected to show its first touch-screen MacBook Pro — so today’s M6 refresh is the warm-up act, not the whole show.

What it means for you

For buyers, the decision is now timing, not just specs: the M6 MacBook Pro and iMac arrive November 6 at the same starting prices as before ($1,999 and $1,499), while a potentially bigger MacBook Pro redesign may be revealed within a day. If you need a machine now, the M6 models offer the newest chip and the longest Mac battery life yet; if you can wait a day, see what the October 28 event brings first.
