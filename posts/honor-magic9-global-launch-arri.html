Hollywood's Camera Maker Just Built a Phone: Honor's Magic 9 Goes Global

November 4, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 3 min read

Hollywood's camera maker just built a phone. Honor starts the international rollout of its Magic 9 series in Hong Kong on November 4, 2026, led by the Magic 9 Pro Max — a phone whose camera system was developed with ARRI, the German company behind professional cinema cameras.

What happened

Honor's global chief marketing officer Guan Haitao confirmed the Hong Kong event through a Weibo post, according to Gizmochina, Gizchina, Gagadget and ShiftDelete reporting on November 2–3, 2026. The series was introduced in China on September 28 and has been on sale only there since, with prices starting at 4,499 yuan for the Super Edition. The Hong Kong event is the first step outside China; Honor has confirmed a January 2027 launch for Europe, including the UK and France, but has not announced international pricing or confirmed which models reach every market. The US is not in the announced rollout. The lineup has three models: the compact Magic 9, the camera-focused Magic 9 Pro Max, and the battery-first Super Edition.

Why it matters

The camera is the whole pitch, and the ARRI partnership is the tell. The Pro Max pairs two 200MP sensors — a main camera on a 1/1.28-inch sensor at f/1.57 and a periscope telephoto on a 1/1.4-inch sensor at f/2.6, both stabilised to CIPA 8.0 — with a 50MP ultrawide, Honor's own H1 imaging chip, ARRI LogC3 recording, 14 ARRI Looks presets and an ARRI CINEMA mode at 4K 60fps. The standard Magic 9 carries the same dual 200MP ARRI system in a smaller 6.37-inch body with an 8,000mAh battery and a 55MP square selfie camera. Honor is betting that cinema colour science, not megapixels, is the next phone-camera war — and that a cinema brand on the back is worth more than another sensor spec on a slide.

What it means for you

If you shoot video for work, the Hong Kong event matters for one reason: pricing and availability for the Pro Max will be confirmed there, and that sets the reference price for Europe's January launch. Before upgrading, judge the ARRI Looks on real footage, not launch samples, and test LogC3 grading in your own workflow — a flat profile only pays off if you actually grade. If you are in the UK or France, do not expect a 2026 launch: January is the window, with no exact date or price yet. And if you are in the US, this flagship is not coming to you in this rollout at all.

The Nodrits take

A phone that borrows a cinema brand is selling a look, not just a lens. The question is whether ARRI colour survives contact with everyday light — and if it does, every phone maker will rent a cinema name next.
