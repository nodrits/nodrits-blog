A Company With Zero Employees Is Now Building Websites for $299 a Month

October 25, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 3 min read

A company that says it operates entirely on AI, with zero employees, is now building complete websites for public companies at a flat $299 a month. Widgets & Web announced its AI-operated investor relations websites on October 24, 2026 — and the pitch is a direct challenge to every agency retainer in the business.

What happened

Widgets & Web, a Miami-based investor relations website provider trusted by more than 500 public companies, SPACs and IPO-stage firms, announced complete, custom IR sites designed, built and maintained through AI-driven operations, at a single fixed price of $299 per month, billed annually at $3,588. Every site includes live stock price data, automated SEC filings from EDGAR, press release feeds, investor email alerts, corporate governance pages, and presentations and events sections. Sites are built for ADA and WCAG compliance, with an accessibility widget on every page. The subscription covers design, hosting, security monitoring, and unlimited updates and support, with no setup fees, no design fees and no per-change charges. The company delivers the first draft of a new IR website within 24 hours of sign-up. Every site runs on Widgets & Web's own proprietary platform — no WordPress, PHP, third-party content management systems or plugin dependencies to maintain. CEO and co-founder Steve Yakubov said: "Public companies have spent decades paying agency retainers and five-figure design bills for websites their investors visit for one thing: accurate, current information."

Why it matters

The announcement splits the website business in two. For sites that are mostly live data plus regular updates — exactly what an investor relations site is — AI operations promise the same upkeep at a flat price, with no retainer and no plugin maintenance. The "zero employees" claim is the provocation to test: the real measure will be filing accuracy, uptime and support quality over months, not the launch press release. But the direction is unmistakable. When a first draft arrives in 24 hours and updates are unlimited and included, the agency model of hourly billing for routine upkeep gets very hard to defend.

What it means for you

If your website is mostly live data plus updates, compare your retainer against the flat rate honestly — and keep a human checking anything regulated, like filings and disclosures. If your site is custom, creative and brand-led, that is where human work still wins and should be priced as premium. Knowing which kind of site you own is now the whole strategy.
