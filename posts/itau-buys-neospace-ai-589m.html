Brazil's Itaú Buys AI Startup NeoSpace for $589 Million

October 26, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 3 min read

A major bank has just bought an AI company outright — not a partnership, not a pilot, a full takeover. Brazilian lender Itaú said on Monday, October 26, 2026, that it has entered an agreement to acquire all shares issued by artificial intelligence startup NeoSpace AI, paying 2.925 billion reais — about $589 million.

What happened

According to Itaú's securities filing, reported by Reuters, the bank will pay part of the 2.925 billion reais at closing and defer the balance over five years. NeoSpace specializes in large AI models, and the two companies are no strangers: NeoSpace has already worked with Itaú for nearly two years. The deal is subject to regulatory approval and customary closing conditions, so it is agreed but not yet closed.

Why it matters

This is a bank deciding that AI capability is too important to rent. After nearly two years of working with NeoSpace's large models, Itaú is bringing the team and the technology in-house rather than continuing as a customer. When a lender pays $589 million for an AI startup, it is a signal that AI models are becoming core infrastructure for finance — the same way a bank would own its core banking system rather than borrow someone else's.

What it means for you

Expect more of your financial tools to be AI-native, built by teams the bank itself owns rather than bolted on from vendors. For businesses, the lesson is the same one Itaú just acted on: if an AI tool becomes central to how you operate, owning the capability beats renting it — and the companies that move first set the terms for everyone else.
