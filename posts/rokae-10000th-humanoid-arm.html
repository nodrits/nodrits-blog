ROKAE Delivers Its 10,000th Humanoid Robot Arm — Robots Are Now Building Robots

October 27, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 3 min read

ROKAE Robotics has delivered its 10,000th humanoid force-controlled arm — and the milestone matters because of where these arms work: five of ROKAE's own Helios wheeled humanoids, fitted with the same arms, already handle parts, assembly and inspection on a factory line that builds humanoid robot components. Robots building robots is no longer a demo video.

What happened

ROKAE Robotics (HKEX: 3752) announced the delivery on October 26, 2026, via PR Newswire from Beijing. The milestone unit rolled off the company's manufacturing base in Shandong Province in September. The 10,000 figure covers the company's humanoid force-controlled arm product line alone — separate from its industrial and collaborative robots, which are deployed across more than 40 countries and regions. More than 80 industrial partners have integrated the arms into production applications, according to company figures.

Why it matters

Embodied AI has spent years in demo videos. A 10,000-unit delivery count is the moment it becomes a manufacturing story instead: repeatable hardware, repeatable performance, real factory floors. The key technology is force control at every joint — the arm senses how hard it pushes, pulls and grips, which is what lets a robot handle real parts without crushing them. As founder and CEO Tuo Hua put it: “There are no shortcuts in force control. Reaching 10,000 units means delivering consistent performance across hardware, control algorithms and manufacturing processes at scale.”

What it means for you

If you run production, the lesson is to pilot one repeatable task — a handling or assembly job that never changes — and measure damage rates and cycle time before and after. Keep humans on judgment calls and exceptions, and demand service, spares and safety data before scaling anything across a line. The robots are ready for the boring, repeatable work; the judgment work still belongs to people.
