The AI Assistant That Never Sleeps: Anthropic's Claude Duet Works Even After You Close the App

October 22, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 4 min read

Anthropic has introduced Claude Duet, an always-on AI assistant that keeps working on its own cloud computer even after you close the app — checking, drafting and following up without waiting for your next prompt. It is rolling out as a research preview to Claude Pro and Max subscribers in the US, through a waitlist.

What happened

Anthropic announced Claude Duet, a proactive AI agent built on its Opus 5.5 model, according to the Dosa AI daily brief. Unlike a normal chatbot that only responds when you type something, Duet runs continuously on its own cloud-based computer, so it can keep working in the background while you do something else — or nothing at all. As 9to5Mac reported, Duet uses the same memories, skills and connectors as regular Claude, and it can search your past conversations so you don't have to repeat yourself. It also plugs into tools like Stripe, where it can check for unpaid invoices and draft payment reminders on its own. Access is limited for now: a research preview for US-based Pro and Max users on a waitlist. Team and Enterprise plans are not included yet.

Why it matters

AI assistants are shifting from "answers when asked" to "acts on its own." That is a much bigger change than a new chat feature. An agent that follows up invoices, monitors things and drafts work in the background is a glimpse of where everyday business automation is heading — and, as the Dosa brief notes, it changes how permissions and guardrails have to be designed, because an AI that acts without a prompt needs clear limits on what it may do alone. Every major AI lab is racing in this direction; Duet is Anthropic's move.

What it means for you

If you're a Claude Pro or Max subscriber in the US, you can join the waitlist and try an assistant that keeps working after you log off. And here is the catch: Duet counts toward your existing plan usage limits, and because it works all the time, it can eat through those limits faster than normal chat — 9to5Mac points out users may hit their caps sooner. For everyone outside the US or on team plans, this is a preview of what's coming to your workflow soon: software that doesn't just respond, but takes initiative.

The Nodrits take

Proactive agents are exactly the kind of AI workflow we build toward: systems that quietly handle follow-ups, checks and drafts so people can focus on real work.
