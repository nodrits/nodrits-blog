Google's Strongest AI Is Open to Everyone — But the Price Doubles Soon

November 11, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 3 min read

Google's strongest AI model is now open to everyone who pays — but the cheap price has an expiry date. On November 10, 2026, Google DeepMind made Gemini 4 Carbon available to paying developers, enterprises and Google AI Ultra subscribers, two weeks after it first reached cybersecurity defenders through the Fairwind Program.

What happened

Google describes Carbon as its strongest model, built for cybersecurity capabilities and long-horizon agentic workflows — complex knowledge work, software engineering and scientific reasoning that runs for hours or days. It is the second Gemini 4 frontier model, following Gemini 4 Argon, which Google says remains the better choice for most software engineering and knowledge work. Carbon is available through the paid Gemini API in AI Studio and Android Studio, Gemini for business, and for AI Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search and Antigravity. Google is also previewing '/swarm' in Antigravity, a feature that coordinates several AI agents at once. Pricing is introductory: $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens through November 30, 2026, doubling to $4 and $20 from December 1. Carbon is not available on the free API tier. Google is giving 10,000 scientists free access to Carbon for six months. On independent figures, the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (version 5) scored Carbon at 59 and ranked it first, one point ahead of Claude Opus 5.5; Google's own benchmarks place it among the top models, behind Anthropic's Fable 5.2.

Why it matters

The frontier-model race is now a pricing race too. Google is opening its strongest model with a half-price window that closes in three weeks — a classic land-grab: get developers building on Carbon now, before the meter doubles. And the positioning matters: Carbon is not a general chatbot upgrade, it is aimed at long, multi-step work and cybersecurity, the two places where AI agents either earn their keep or fail in public.

What it means for you

If you build with AI, the next three weeks are the cheapest Carbon will ever be — test it on your longest, hardest tasks now, while the introductory price holds. But budget for December: API prices doubling is the new normal for frontier models, so price your own AI services on the post-intro rate, not the teaser. And if you sell AI work to clients, long-horizon agent capability is the pitch to learn — the model that finishes a multi-hour job is worth more than the one that answers fast.
