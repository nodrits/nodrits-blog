Blacksmith Hits $1B: The Startup That Tests AI-Written Code

October 23, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 3 min read

AI writes the code now — so who checks it? On Oct 21, 2026, Blacksmith answered with a $66 million Series C led by Sequoia Capital at a $1 billion valuation, making the two-year-old cloud CI platform a unicorn.

What happened

Blacksmith builds continuous-integration infrastructure that helps companies build and test software — including code written by AI agents. The Series C comes just two months after a $45M Series B at a $550M valuation, and a year after a $10M Series A at $60M. Total funding: $125M. The company reports 17x annual recurring revenue growth and more than 90 million automated CI jobs run, with customers including Mercury and Granola. Co-founder and CEO Aditya Jayaprakash told TechCrunch the company exists to make CI "less painful": "As agents write more of the world's software, the question that matters is whether it can be trusted."

Why it matters

The bottleneck in software development just moved. Writing code is becoming the cheap part; verifying it is the scarce, valuable part. Investors are now paying unicorn prices for trust infrastructure.

What it means for you

If you use AI coding assistants, your competitive edge isn't speed — it's verified quality. Test ruthlessly, and make testing your billable differentiator: clients will pay a premium for AI-assisted work they can trust.

The Nodrits take

Notice what the unicorn is: not a code-writing AI, but a code-checking one. In every gold rush the winners sell shovels — in the AI coding gold rush, they sell shovels and smoke detectors.
