Oura Buys AI Health Startup Counsel Health in $625M Deal

November 11, 2026 · Nodrits Solutions · 3 min read

Oura is buying its way from tracking your health to treating it. The smart-ring maker announced on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, that it is acquiring Counsel Health — an AI-native primary care company — for $625 million, in a deal that puts a medical AI and real physicians inside the Oura app.

What happened

Oura announced the acquisition on Tuesday, November 10, 2026, just weeks after postponing its planned IPO of up to $2.2 billion indefinitely, citing uncertainty in the IPO market. Counsel Health is an AI-powered healthcare platform that combines a chat-based medical experience with access to licensed physicians. Its service was already integrated into the Oura app in June, letting users get personalized medical advice from Counsel's medical AI and chat with a physician when needed — all inside the Oura app. Following the acquisition, Counsel will keep serving its existing and new enterprise partners, and its CEO, Muthu Alagappan, will join Oura as chief clinical AI officer along with the company's 70 employees. The deal is expected to close before the end of 2027. Counsel Health had raised $25 million in a Series A a year ago, led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) and GV, and PitchBook estimates it had raised $36 million in total.

Why it matters

The deal turns Oura from a tracker into a care provider. Oura has dominated the smart-ring market for years, but rivals like RingConn and Ultrahuman are pushing in — and a ring that only reports your sleep score is a much weaker product than a ring that can answer a health question, arrange a prescription or book a referral. Oura says the two companies plan to expand across preventative care, acute care, and specialty and chronic care.

"Oura helps members understand their physiology and, with Counsel, they can take action on their health across a range of needs, from quick questions and prescriptions to lab results, chronic care, and referrals," said Oura CEO Tom Hale in the announcement.
The numbers behind the move are strong: Oura's revenue grew 153% year-over-year to $533 million for the quarter ended September 30, and its fiscal 2026 revenue reached approximately $1.75 billion, up 92% year-over-year. The company reached an $11 billion valuation after a $900 million late-stage funding round in October 2025.

What it means for you

If you wear a smart ring or build health products, watch this shift: the wearable market is moving from measurement to medical action. For users, the practical change is simple — the same app that tells you your readiness score could soon connect you to a doctor who sees your actual biometric trends. For health-tech founders, it is a signal that distribution through a device people already wear daily is now worth more than a standalone care app. And for anyone skeptical of AI medical advice, Counsel's model keeps physicians in the loop for clinical decisions — a detail worth watching as the integration rolls out.
